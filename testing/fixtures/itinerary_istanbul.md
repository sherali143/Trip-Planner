# 🧳 COMPREHENSIVE TRAVEL TIPS & LOCAL KNOWLEDGE

### **1. Entry Requirements & Documentation:**
- **Passport:** Must have at least 6 months validity remaining beyond August 19, 2026.
- **e-Visa:** Pakistani passport holders with a valid Schengen, US, UK, or Ireland visa/residence permit can apply for a quick Turkish e-Visa online at [evisa.gov.tr](https://www.evisa.gov.tr). Ensure printed copies are kept with your passport.

### **2. Money, Currency & Tipping:**
- **Currency:** Turkish Lira (TRY). Cash is helpful for small street vendors or simit carts, but credit/debit cards (Visa/Mastercard) are accepted at 98% of restaurants and shops.
- **Tipping:** 10% is standard in mid-range and fine dining restaurants. For exceptional fine dining, 10–15% is appreciated.

### **3. Local Transport & Navigation:**
- **Istanbulkart:** A contactless public transport card valid for trams, metros, and public ferries. Buy and charge one at any major tram station (such as Karaköy or Sultanahmet).
- **Taxis & Rideshare:** Use **Uber** or the local app **BiTaksi** to hail official yellow/turquoise taxis; this avoids taxi meter scams or negotiation friction.

### **4. Cultural Etiquette & Dress Code:**
- **Mosque Etiquette:** Remove shoes at entry. Men should wear trousers covering the knees. Women should cover shoulders, knees, and hair (bring a lightweight silk scarf in your day bag).
- **Hospitality:** Accepting a cup of Turkish tea (*Çay*) offered by shopkeepers is a warm gesture of hospitality; you are under no obligation to purchase goods.

### **5. What to Pack:**
- **Footwear:** Very comfortable, stylish walking shoes or sneakers (Istanbul is hilly with ancient cobblestone streets).
- **Clothing:** Breathable linen shirts, lightweight cotton trousers, elegant smart-casual dinner wear for rooftops like Mikla and Sunset Grill.
- **Electronics:** European Type C / F plug adapter (230V, 50Hz).
- **Sun Protection:** High-SPF sunscreen, sunglasses, and a wide-brim hat for daytime walking tours.
